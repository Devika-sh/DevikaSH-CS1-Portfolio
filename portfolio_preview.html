A little about me

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science student at Mar Baselios College of Engineering and Technology with a practical, hands-on interest in UI/UX and graphic design. Over the past couple of years I've moved from designing internal software UI, to freelance app and landing-page design for a fintech startup, to leading visual branding for college-wide events and HR-tech community initiatives.
